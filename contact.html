✦ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. We offer expedited 2-day shipping for an additional fee. All orders over $75 receive free standard shipping.

Are your products prayed over?

Yes! Every single product is prayed over by our ministry team before it leaves our facility. We believe what we ship carries a genuine blessing.

What is your return policy?

We offer a full 30-day return on all items in original condition. Contact us at hello@getrightministries.org and we'll make it right.

Can I submit a prayer request?

Absolutely. Use the Divine Messenger widget on this page, or call our prayer line at +1 (555) 987-PRAY. Our prayer team personally intercedes for every request.

Do you offer ministry partnerships?

Yes! We partner with churches, ministries, and faith-based organizations. Reach out to us at hello@getrightministries.org to discuss partnership opportunities.

Where does my money go?

10% of every purchase directly funds our outreach programs, feeding ministries, and Gospel distribution in underserved communities. Shop and give simultaneously.
