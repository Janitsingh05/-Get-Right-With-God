✦ How We Began

A Ministry Born in Prayer

Get Right With God Ministries was born in 2010 from a simple prayer in a small apartment in Atlanta, Georgia. Our founder, Pastor Emmanuel Williams, felt a divine call to bridge the gap between sacred living and everyday life.

What began as a small table at a church market has grown into a full-fledged online ministry serving over 12,000 faithful souls across the United States and beyond.

Every product we offer is prayed over, carefully selected, and shipped with a blessing — because we believe that the sacred should touch every corner of your life.
